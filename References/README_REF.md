# References

This research is based on established bibliometric and scientometric literature related to conference evaluation, citation analysis, and research impact assessment. The following references provide the theoretical foundation for the proposed **Conference Excellence Index (CEI)**.

## References

1. **P. Makvandi, A. Nodehi, and F. R. Tay**, *"Conference accreditation and need of a bibliometric measure to distinguish predatory conferences,"* **Publications**, vol. 9, no. 2, Art. no. 16, 2021.

2. **X. Li, W. Rong, H. Shi, J. Tang, and Z. Xiong**, *"The impact of conference ranking systems in computer science: A comparative regression analysis,"* **Scientometrics**, vol. 116, no. 2, pp. 879–907, 2018.

3. **W. S. Martins, M. A. Gonçalves, A. H. F. Laender, and N. Ziviani**, *"Assessing the quality of scientific conferences based on bibliographic citations,"* **Scientometrics**, vol. 83, no. 1, pp. 133–155, 2010.

4. **J. E. Hirsch**, *"An index to quantify an individual's scientific research output,"* **Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences**, vol. 102, no. 46, pp. 16569–16572, 2005.

## Citation

If you use this repository or build upon the proposed **Conference Excellence Index (CEI)**, please cite the associated research paper and acknowledge the original bibliometric studies listed above.
